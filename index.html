These are kept on the slideshow itself (the PowerPoint Files folder on its SD card). A USB stick plugged into the slideshow has its PowerPoints copied in here, then the stick can come out. The slideshow's own Wi-Fi
Fast upload, for big PowerPoints: the slideshow switches its Wi-Fi to a network of its own for a while (same name and password every time, so your phone joins it by itself after the first time). Upload on the page, then tap "I'm finished" — it goes back to its normal Wi-Fi then, or by itself after 10 minutes of nothing happening. Uploads take seconds instead of minutes. With a USB Wi-Fi adapter plugged in, the adapter keeps the internet and the Pi's built-in Wi-Fi does this.
Networks you join here are saved on the slideshow (its wifi.txt), so it joins them by itself next time. A wifi.txt on a USB stick plugged into the slideshow is added to it too. The Wi-Fi password is sent encrypted.

Send a PowerPoint by Bluetooth

Pair your Android phone or Windows laptop with the slideshow once, then send it .pptx files.
While pairing is open, pair with Slideshow in your phone's or laptop's Bluetooth settings, and check the number on the screen matches. Then share/send the PowerPoint to it by Bluetooth. It's saved on the slideshow and starts playing. (iPhones can't send files to non-Apple devices.)
