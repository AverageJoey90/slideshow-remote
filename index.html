Send a PowerPoint by Bluetooth

Pair your Android phone or Windows laptop with the slideshow once, then send it .pptx files.
While pairing is open, pair with Slideshow in your phone's or laptop's Bluetooth settings, and check the number on the screen matches. Then share/send the PowerPoint to it by Bluetooth. It's saved onto the USB stick and starts playing. (iPhones can't send files to non-Apple devices.)
